The period essentials guide

Menstrual products, explained

Learn how different products work, what they are designed for and how to care for them. There is no one product that suits everyone.

♡

Choose what works for you. Comfort, flow, access, budget and personal preference can all matter. You can use different products at different times.

Always follow the instructions on the product packaging. For tampons, use the lowest absorbency you need and change them every 4–8 hours; never leave one in for more than 8 hours. If you feel suddenly unwell while using a period product, remove an internal product if you can and seek urgent medical advice. Ask a trusted adult or healthcare professional if you are unsure what is right for you.

A few useful reminders

Comfort matters more than labels

01

Try different styles

Soft, wired, non-wired, padded and unpadded styles all feel different. There is no single right choice.

02

Check how it feels

A comfortable band should feel secure without digging in. Straps should stay in place without needing to be very tight.

03

Your body can change

Fit can change over time. It is normal to need a different size or style later.
